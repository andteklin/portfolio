Hi, my name is Andrew, and I'm a fourth-year Electrical and Computer Engineering student at Cornell. I'm passionate about building innovative solutions at the intersection of hardware and software. My experience spans embedded systems, robotics, and AI hardware engineering, with a focus on creating systems that interact intelligently with humans and the physical world. I'm committed to creating technologies that are practical, accessible, and sustainable.

Outside of ECE

Outside of my academic and professional interests, I enjoy leading worship through music, playing guitar and piano with my campus ministry and church. I also enjoy fixing computers and repurposing old hardware. These experiences have shaped how I think about teamwork, intentionality, and building technologies that serve people well.
