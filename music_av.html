Music & AV

I love seeing how ECE intersects with music and AV.

Outside of engineering, I serve as a Worship Leader for Cru, a campus ministry at Cornell. I work with two other leaders to plan music, lead team meetings, and help others steward their talents well. This experience has helped me grow in accountability, navigating through difficult conversations, and lead by example in a way that reflets care and honors the mission of the body.

As I continue in my undergraduate career, I aim to continue growing in leadership and teamwork by taking on roles to support others, communicate effectively across disciplines, and leading with empathy and intentionality
